## The one-sentence diagnosis

Run our own scoring engine against our own site and it returns **a score of 15.0 — the prior —
with zero findings and zero counter-evidence.** Our corpus has four counter-evidence rules
(`counter.licensed-foundry-face`, `counter.real-photography`, `counter.handmade-artifact`,
`counter.anti-spam-plumbing`) and **our site earns none of them.** We built an instrument that
measures human investment in a page, pointed it at ourselves, and it found nothing to measure. That
is the founder's sentence, in our own units.

# BROKEN — information missing, or it does not work

Ranked by how much a first-time visitor loses.

### B1. The fold's self-scan renders a header, a footer, and nothing in between
**Surface:** `/` · `prod-1440-home.png`, `prod-390-home.png`, `dark-home-fold.png`
The card reads `slop-scorer.vercel.app · scan_ui · 0 of 39 checks` / `0 FINDINGS · 0.0 s to run · 0 s ago`
and then **stops**. `SURFACES.md` §B specifies "four visible check rows in the exact `item` layout the
receipt uses". Zero rows render. The single most important object on the site is an empty rectangle
with a boast in it.
`0 FINDINGS` next to `0 of 39 checks` is also self-refuting: it is not a clean scan, it is *no* scan,
and any reader who reads left to right sees that.
**Fix (beyond the in-flight scan repair):** the card must never render `N findings` when
`evaluated.length === 0`. Substitute the receipt's own abstention treatment —
`NOT ASSESSED · 0 of 39 checks ran` and the terminal sentence in the body, per `SURFACES.md` §"If the
scan endpoint is down". And when it *does* run, render four rows: the honest four are the checks that
ran and found nothing, with `ran against the rendered page, nothing to cite`. The `Frame` already
supports this; the `quiet` slice in `self-scan-card.tsx` just never gets a non-empty `evaluated`.

### B2. `/receipt/self` — the nav's own "Our own receipt" — is an entirely empty page
**Surface:** `/receipt/self` · `prod-1440-receipt-self.png`
Headline: **"0.0 seconds"**. Body: `NOT CONFIGURED · NO REPRODUCTION WAS ATTEMPTED`. Findings:
`0 citations · 0 inferred`. Abstention band: *"Playwright is not installed on this server"* and
*"39 of the 39 rules in this corpus did not run"*. Arithmetic table: two rows, both `0.0`.

zero for a run that never started is a false measurement. Replace the H1 with `We could not scan
ourselves on this deploy.` per `SURFACES.md` §8's all-inconclusive rule, and drop "Playwright" for
"no browser is available on this host".

### B3. Three of the four sample receipts contain no figure and no findings
**Surface:** `/receipt/8B10-2D`, `/receipt/C7E3-51`, `/receipt/A05E-13` ·
`prod-1440-r-8B10.png`, `prod-1440-r-C7E3.png`, `prod-1440-r-A05E.png`

### B4. The arithmetic table is visually broken on every assessed receipt
**Surface:** `/receipt/4F2A-9C` and every `assessed` receipt · **`zoom-arith-table.png`**
Each family row's `caveat` text renders as one unwrapped ~1,900 px line that runs **underneath** the
`FINDINGS`, `POINTS` and `CAP` cells and off the right edge of the table, truncated mid-sentence
("…in one release, so this ▸"). Three rows do it. The numbers `1`, `+37.0`, `not reached` sit on top of
running text. It reads as a rendering failure, which on a page whose entire argument is "check our
arithmetic" is the worst possible place for one.
**Cause:** `apps/web/components/receipt/arithmetic.tsx:84` — `max-w-[38ch]` on a `<TableCell>`.
`max-width` on a `<td>` is ignored under `table-layout: auto`.
**Fix:** wrap the cell contents in a block that *can* take the constraint:
`<TableCell><div className="max-w-[38ch]">{family.title}<span className="block …">{family.caveat}</span></div></TableCell>`,
or set `table-fixed` on the `<Table>` with explicit column widths. Verify at 1440 and 390 — the
`overflow-x-auto` wrapper currently hides the defect from a page-level overflow check, which is why
nothing caught it.

# UGLY — why it reads as unfinished

### U1. The typeface never loads. The entire visual identity is the OS default.
**Surface:** every route. Measured via `document.fonts`, in the browser, on production:
```
loadedFonts: ["Slop Fallback Sans 400 500 loaded", "Slop Fallback Mono 400 500 loaded"]
h1 font-family: "ABC Diatype", "Slop Fallback Sans", ui-sans-serif, …
```
`ABC Diatype` is first in the stack and **is not shipped** — `apps/web/public/` contains two SVGs and
nothing else, and every `@font-face` for Diatype is commented out in `globals.css`. Both fallbacks are
pure `local()` references, so on Windows every headline on this site is **Segoe UI** and every mono
block is Cascadia/Consolas.
`SELF-AUDIT.md` §4 called this precisely: *"**Diatype is unbought.** … a system-stack site will read
more generic than the mock. **Do not launch in the interim state and call it the design.**"* We
launched in the interim state.
In a design with zero imagery, zero colour, zero radius and zero shadow, **type is 100% of the
identity** — and it is the same type as every untouched `create-next-app`. This is the largest single
contributor to "looks like shit", it is invisible in a code review, and no amount of layout work
compensates for it.
**Fix, in priority order:** (1) buy the Dinamo licence, self-host the four woff2s, uncomment the
`@font-face` blocks. (2) If the licence cannot be bought this week, ship a *different licensed or
genuinely distinctive* face rather than the system stack — the Lark comparables lesson was "funded
human sites buy a real typeface", and the corpus rule `counter.licensed-foundry-face` awards −0.8
for exactly that. Right now we score nothing on our own counter-rule. **Do not** ship a Trial/DEMO
build; the code comment is right that this is a named tell.

### U2. The flagship figure is two identical clip-art wireframes
**Surface:** `/` and `/receipt/4F2A-9C` · `prod-1440-home.png` (figure at y≈790)
The only two images on the site are `4F2A-9C-submitted.svg` (3,390 B) and
`4F2A-9C-recreation.svg` (3,387 B) — hand-authored SVG wireframes that differ only in gradient ID,
`<title>`, and three card line-lengths. Side by side at any size they are **indistinguishable**. So the
claim "8.4 seconds to remake this" has no visual payoff at all; the panels read as a copy-paste, not
as a feat.
Compounding it: the plates are a **violet-to-blue gradient**, which is the single most saturated object
on the entire site and, in dark mode, the only colour on the screen (`prod-1440d-home.png`). Anyone who
screenshots our homepage gets a violet-blue gradient in frame — the artifact our own top-weighted
visual rule (`css.violet-blue-gradient`) exists to name.
**Fix:** the recreation must *visibly* be a rebuild, not a duplicate — different copy, different photo
crop, honest line-break differences at a glance, so the reader's eye does the comparing. And the
sample artifact should be a real rendered page (we can make one, screenshot it, and own it) rather
than a vector diagram of a page. A diagram of a website is not a website.

### U3. The export PNG — the one asset built to travel — is the ugliest thing we make
**Surface:** `GET /api/receipt/4F2A-9C/export?size=square` · **`export-square.png`**
- ~40% of each panel is **flat grey letterbox** above and below the 1024×640 plate stuffed into a
  square slot. It reads as a broken-image placeholder.
- The disclaimer band is set in a **blocky bitmap face, ALL CAPS, six solid lines, no leading**. It is
  legally compliant (same frame, same size, present) and typographically it looks like a 1994 BBS dump.
- Both panels are the identical wireframe (U2), so the image makes no argument.
**Fix:** crop to the plate's real aspect instead of letterboxing (use `size=wide`/16:9 as the default
share size and letterbox nothing); set the disclaimer in mixed case with real leading; render the band
in the same face the site uses. Then re-run the OCR height assertion. This asset is the product's
distribution — it should be the best-looking thing we ship, and it is currently the worst.

### U4. At 1440 the page is a narrow column with a 500 px void beside it
**Surface:** every route · `prod-1440-*.png`
Content stops at roughly x=940 in a 1440 viewport. The H1 is capped at 940 px, body at 760 px, and
H2s at **24ch** — so "A real receipt, with the part our lawyer cares about visible before you sign up"
wraps to **three lines** while 500 px of empty page sits to its right. The receipt card, the evidence
list and the arithmetic table all end around 880 px too. Nothing occupies the right half of the screen
on any page. It does not read as generous whitespace; it reads as a mobile layout that was never
given a desktop.
**Fix:** pick one and commit. Either (a) a real two-column desktop grid — the receipt masthead,
`6 citations · 0 inferred`, the abstention band and the "verify" payloads belong in a right-hand margin
column, which is what an actual document does; or (b) centre the column and *use* the width: let the
figure and the evidence rows go full 1,100 px while prose stays at 72ch. Raise the H2 cap from 24ch to
~34ch either way — three-line H2s at 32px are the main reason the page has no rhythm.

### U5. Uniform section rhythm — our own rule `struct.uniform-section-rhythm`, on our own page
**Surface:** `/` · `prod-1440-home.png`
Four sections, `gap-24` between every one, each opening with an H2 at the identical left margin,
identical size, identical weight, followed by an identical `max-w-[72ch]` muted paragraph, followed by
an identical accent underline link. There is **no focal point anywhere on the page** — nothing is
bigger, closer, darker, warmer or interrupted. The eye has no reason to stop.
**Fix:** break the grid deliberately once. Full-bleed the receipt figure to the page edge with a
`--surface` background behind it and no side padding, so one block on the page is a different shape
from all the others. Reduce the gap between the H2 and its paragraph, and increase it between
sections, so the page reads as four groups rather than eight evenly-spaced things.

### U6. `/method` is a 12,133 px wall with no way in
**Surface:** `/method` · `method-y0.png`, `method-y4000.png`, `method-y8000.png`, `method-y11000.png`
3,777 words, **zero images**, 39 rule cards with identical internal structure
(id · weight · title · explanation · "When it is wrong:" · "How to not do this:"), no table of
contents, no family jump-nav, no sticky rule index, no back-to-top. Receipts deep-link into the middle
of it (`/method#dom.uniform-cards`) and drop the reader into an undifferentiated scroll with no
orientation.
The writing is the best content on the site and almost nobody will reach the middle of it.
**Fix:** a sticky left rail listing the seven families with counts, jumping to each. A one-screen
summary table at the top (`id · family · weight · severity`) that links down. And a visible
`← back to the receipt` when arrived via a hash.

### U7. Mobile nav wraps into a cramped block
**Surface:** 390 px, every route · `prod-390-home.png`
The wordmark `SLOP SCORER` breaks onto two lines and each of the three nav items wraps
independently, producing a ragged six-line header before any content. The self-scan card header wraps
into three lines. The `input-group` overflows its container by 4–5 px at both widths (measured:
`scrollWidth 346` vs `clientWidth 342`).
**Fix:** `whitespace-nowrap` on the wordmark and each nav item; collapse the nav to a single row of
short labels (`Method · Receipts · Self`) below 640 px; `min-w-0` on the input-group's flex parent.

### U8. The founder's note is set as legal small print
**Surface:** footer, every route
*"Built by one person who got tired of arguing about whether something was made by a machine, and
decided to measure how long it takes to remake it instead."* — this is the one honest human line the
whole corpus says to lead with, and it is 14 px muted grey in the footer, unsigned, unnamed, with no
face, no handwriting and no date.
**Fix:** promote it out of the footer into a real block above it, set at body size in `--ink`, with a
name and a date. See A1.

# Section 3, answered directly: do we exhibit our own tells?

We claim `0 findings`. Honestly assessed against our own 39-rule corpus:

**Rules that fire on us right now:**
- **`builder.bare-platform-domain`** (weight 1.0, medium): *"A production site on a raw `*.vercel.app`
  host has skipped the one step that costs money and takes a human decision: a domain."* We are on
  `slop-scorer.vercel.app`. **Our own rule fires on our own address bar while our own fold says
  `0 findings`.** `SELF-AUDIT.md` §1 named this exact risk: *"shipping a demo on a bare preview URL for
  a launch tweet."*
- **`craft.scaffold-title`** is at risk via B10 — the 404's title is identical to `/`'s.

**Counter-evidence rules we earn: none of four.**
- `counter.licensed-foundry-face` (−0.8): requires a self-hosted `@font-face` for a non-free face.
  We ship two `local()` fallbacks and no font files. **Does not fire.**
- `counter.real-photography` (−0.5): requires ≥3 `photographic` images with ≥15-char alt. We have
  **two images site-wide, both SVG**, `photographic: false`, and zero images on seven of nine routes.
  **Does not fire.**
- `counter.handmade-artifact` (−0.5): a drawn mark, grain, photographed handwriting, a torn-paper
  plate. We have none. **Does not fire.**
- `counter.anti-spam-plumbing` (−0.4): our form does not submit. **Does not fire.**

**So is "0 findings" true?** No — it is true only in the sense that no check ran. And once checks do
run, the honest number is not zero. More damningly: **our engine has nothing to say about us in either
direction.** We would land on the bare prior. The rules cannot see our particular failure because our
particular failure is *absence* — no type investment, no photography, no artifact — and absence is
what three of our four counter-rules were written to detect. **The corpus already diagnosed this site.
We just never pointed it at ourselves and read the counter column.**

**What our rules cannot see, and a hostile reviewer can:** that the H1 is Segoe UI; that the only
colour on the page is a violet-blue gradient; that the two panels of the flagship figure are the same
drawing; that the arithmetic table renders on top of itself; that seven of nine routes contain no
image at all. Not one of those is encodable by the 39 rules as written. `SELF-AUDIT.md` §Risk 2 said
to ship a permanent **"what our own scan cannot see"** section — the `/` section by that name exists
but lists only *modalities* (motion, audio, typography-in-image). It should list the five specific
things above, by name, about this site. Volunteering them is the only credible position, and it is
free.

# Section 4: the missing non-UI brand artifact

`SELF-AUDIT.md` §Risk 1 predicted the whole failure and proposed the fix: *"the missing piece is the
one non-UI brand artifact every human comparable has… My candidate: the **printed physical receipt** —
actually print one, photograph it, and let that photograph be the only image on the site."*

It is still the right answer, and there is now a second reason to do it that the self-audit did not
have: **our own corpus pays for it twice.** A photographed printed receipt fires
`counter.real-photography` *and* `counter.handmade-artifact`. We would be following our own published
rule, in public, and could say so on the page — which converts a design decision into an argument,
which is the only kind of design this product is allowed to make.

**Candidates, ranked:**

1. **The printed receipt, photographed.** Print `4F2A-9C` on real thermal or tractor-feed paper.
   Photograph it on a desk, in real light, with the curl in the paper and a thumb holding it flat.
   Make it the hero image and the `og:image`. Cost: an afternoon and a $30 receipt printer. It is
   literally the product's name made physical, it cannot be generated, and it dates the brand to a
   person and a room.
2. **The corpus, as a wall.** Photograph the 39 rules written out by hand — index cards on a wall,
   or a marked-up printout of the Lark hygiene script that the corpus was derived from, with the
   crossings-out visible. This is Amata's photographed-handwriting move and it doubles as provenance:
   it shows the rules came from somewhere.
3. **The failure photograph.** Photograph a screen showing a scan that we got *wrong*, annotated by
   hand. Highest-integrity option, hardest to fake, and the single most on-brand image the company
   could own.
4. **A signed founder note with a real signature scan.** Cheapest of the four; weakest alone, strong
   as the caption under (1).

Do (1) this week. It closes the "no imagery" gap, the "no focal point" gap, the "nothing designed by
a person" gap and two counter-evidence rules in one photograph.

# Section 5: content quality — explaining where it should be showing

The writing is genuinely good — better than the design by a wide margin — and that is part of the
problem: **every mechanic on this site is described in a paragraph that a five-second demonstration
would beat.** Measured word counts: `/` 567, `/method` 3,777, receipts ~500 each. Images: 2, total.

# The five changes that would most change a first impression

1. **Ship a real typeface.** (U1) Buy Diatype, self-host the four woff2s, uncomment the `@font-face`
   blocks. Every headline on this site is currently Segoe UI. In a design with no imagery, no colour,
   no radius and no shadow, type *is* the design, and ours is the operating system's. Nothing else on
   this list matters as much and nothing else is as invisible in a code review.
2. **Print a receipt, photograph it, make it the hero image.** (Section 4) The site has two images and
   both are clip-art. This one photograph fixes "no imagery", "no focal point", "nothing designed by a
   person", and fires two of our own counter-evidence rules — which we then say on the page.
3. **Make the fold actually run and actually show four rows — and let it report the finding we
   genuinely have.** (B1, Section 3) `0 of 39 checks · 0 FINDINGS` is the worst sentence on the site.
   Replace it with a real run showing four cited rows, one of which is
   `builder.bare-platform-domain — this site is served from a bare vercel.app`, with a line saying we
   are publishing it rather than fixing it first. `SELF-AUDIT.md` §Risk 2 asked for exactly this: a
   page that scores perfectly on its own tool is less believable than one that scores 1 and explains it.
   It converts the biggest liability on the site into its best asset for the cost of one sentence.
4. **Fix the arithmetic table and give every sample receipt a figure.** (B4, B3) One is a visible
   rendering bug on the page whose whole argument is "check our arithmetic"; the other quadruples the
   site's imagery and turns three walls of prose into three demonstrations. Together they are the
   fastest route to "the information is there".
5. **Use the desktop width and break the rhythm once.** (U4, U5) Raise the H2 cap from 24ch, stop the
   three-line headings, full-bleed the receipt figure so exactly one block on the page is a different
   shape from every other, and let the evidence rows use the width they have. The page currently has
   no focal point at any viewport; this gives it one without adding a single new element.

**Runner-up, cheapest thing on the list:** fix `/receipt/self` or take "Our own receipt" out of the
nav. Right now the site's third navigation item leads to a page whose headline is `0.0 seconds` and
whose body says nothing ran. That is the founder's sentence, one click from the front door.
